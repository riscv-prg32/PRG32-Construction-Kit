# Classes

*Notice version 1.0, from 2026-10-21.*

**In short:** teachers and facilitators of your class can open your games to help you. They
can read them but not change or publish them.

- A teacher or administrator adds you to a class. The class has a name, for example "3B",
  and lists who is in it.
- Teachers of that class see your username and your games. Other students do not.
- Leaving the class, or deleting your account, removes you from it.

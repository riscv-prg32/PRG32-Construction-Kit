# Quick start (English)

Other languages: [Italiano](quickstart.it.md) · [Español](quickstart.es.md) ·
[Français](quickstart.fr.md) · [Deutsch](quickstart.de.md) · [简体中文](quickstart.zh-Hans.md)

1. **Open the kit** in a browser. Choose your language with the menu at the top
   (English, Italiano, Español, Français, Deutsch, 简体中文).
2. **No account?** Press *Try without an account*: your games stay in this browser only.
3. **New Project.** Choose how to write it: **Blocks**, **C**, **Assembly (RISC-V)** or
   **Python (PRG32 Python)**. Give it a title and press *Create*.
4. **Run** (Shift+F10) builds the game and starts it in the simulator on the right. Click the
   screen, then use the arrow keys (A and B are Z and X).
5. **Generate** (Ctrl+Shift+G) shows the C and the RISC-V assembly the kit makes, explained line
   by line. They are the same in every language of the interface.
6. **Compile** (Ctrl+Shift+B, needs an account) makes the `.prg32` cartridge to download, to
   play on a PRG32 console or in PRG32-QT.
7. **Debug**: open the *Debug* tab, press a line number to stop there, then *Next line*.
8. **Your data**: the footer link *Privacy & your data* says what the kit keeps and why.

Teachers: the tutorials in [docs/tutorials](.) go further (catch game, sprites, sound,
publishing, debugger, PRG32 Python).

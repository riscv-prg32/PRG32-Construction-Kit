What is running?

The same RV32IMAC interpreter and PRG32 ABI 1.6 as PRG32-QT, compiled to WebAssembly. Guest time counts 33 ms per frame whatever the speed, so a game runs the same at 0.1× and 4×.

Sound is an approximation of PRG32-QT's mixer. See the guide.
